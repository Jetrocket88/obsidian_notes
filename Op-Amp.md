[[Circuit Theory]]

Operational amplifier easy to use for voltage signal amplification, scaling, addition, subtraction, and current-to-voltage conversion.   

Typical Op-Amp gain is very high, usually
$$
A = 10^4 - 10^6V/V
$$
We assume that 
$$
A = \infty
$$

![[Pasted image 20261015102921.png]]

Remember that the output of an op-amp is limited by the supply voltage, this is when the op-amp is saturated.

# Assumed Characteristics

1. Gain is very high, approaching infinity.
2. Input resistance is extremely high 
3. Output resistance is low

$$
R_I = \infty \\
R_{out} = 0 \\
A = \infty \\
A_{input} = B_{input}

$$


# Inverting Example
![[Pasted image 20261015103350.png]]

$$
	A = \frac{R_f}{R_i} \\[10pt]
	A = \frac{V_{in}}{V_{out}} \\[10pt]
	\therefore \\
	\frac{R_f}{R_i} = \frac{V_{in}}{V_{out}}(?) \\
$$
