[[Circuit Theory]]
The value of power consumed by the load varies with load resistance, RL.  To determine the value of RL that maximises load power, PL must be differentiated with respect to RL and set to zero. 

![[Pasted image 20261015103923.png]]

# How do we extract the most power from a source?

![[Pasted image 20261015103957.png]]


Power in the load is greatest when the load resistance equals the Thévenin Resistance.
# Example

![[Pasted image 20261015104235.png]]


Calculate Rth and Vth and Rth is the answer.
Rth is 4kohms
Vth is 6V
The maximum power is just 
$$
P_{max} = \frac{V^2_{TH}}{4R_{TH}} \\[10pt]
P_{max} = \frac{6^2}{4 * 4000} \\[10pt]
P_{max} = \frac{36}{16000} \\[10pt]
P_{max} = 2.25mW \\[10pt]
$$
