What is the formula for electrical conductivity related to resistivity?
?
$$
\sigma = \frac{1}{p} 
$$
sigma, conductivity, siemens per meter (S/m)
p, electrical resistivity, Ohm-meters 

What is the formula for electrical resistivity related to conductivity?
?
$$
p = \frac{1}{\sigma}
$$
sigma, conductivity, siemens per meter (S/m)
p, electrical resistivity, Ohm-meters 

How are electrical conductivity and electrical resistivity related?
?
They are inverses of each other
